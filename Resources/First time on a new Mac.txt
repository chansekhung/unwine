DECANT - FIRST TIME ON A NEW MAC

1. Drag Decant into the Applications folder.

2. Open Decant.
   macOS may say it "cannot verify" Decant. This is normal for a free app
   that is not sold through Apple. To allow it:
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down. Click "Open Anyway" next to Decant.
     - Type your Mac password.

3. Click "Start setup". Decant downloads the free engine and the Steam bridge
   once (about 430 MB). If you have Apple's Game Porting Toolkit 4 file,
   add it when Decant asks (it is free from Apple with your Apple ID).

4. Steam Play must be on in your Mac Steam. Decant never changes Steam.app.
   See NotProton's project page for how Steam Play gets switched on:
   https://github.com/NotProtonNot/NotProton

5. In Steam, install a Windows game. It shows up in Decant, ready to play.

YOUR GAMES ON A NEW MAC
Steam keeps your games. Copy your Steam library folder over, or let Steam
download them again. Decant's per-game settings are rebuilt in a few clicks.
